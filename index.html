We only get one shot at event day each year.

Hundreds of kids, mentors and parents go through check-in, matching, shopping and checkout in a single day. Once it's over, we usually only have what people remember.

Starting in 2026, we run event day on our own software. Every check-in, match and pickup is saved as it happens, so afterwards we can go back and see how the day actually went.

Each explorer replays one event day, minute by minute. A new one gets added every year, so we can plan the next event off real numbers and see what changed.

How to use it

Every explorer works the same way. Pictures below are from 2026.

  1. Pick Board or StoryBoard puts every chart on one page to dig through. Story walks you through the day one step at a time with Back and Next. Switch between them in the bottom right. Start with Story if it's your first time.
  2. Play with the clockThe bar at the bottom runs the day. Press Play, change the speed, or drag to any time. The orange marks jump straight to the big moments. Every chart moves with it.
  3. Hover anythingEvery dial, dot and line tells you what it is when you hover over it.
  4. Click to dig inClick a kid, group or family and it lights up in every chart. Click a dial for more detail. Press Esc to go back.
  5. Hit i when you're not sureEvery chart has an i in its corner. It explains how to read the chart and what's worth noticing, with times you can click to jump to.
