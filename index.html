SWAB · Event Day Explorer

We only get one shot at event day each year.

Hundreds of kids, mentors and parents go through check-in, matching, shopping and checkout in a single day. Once it's over, we usually only have what people remember.

2026 was the first year we ran event day on our own software. Every check-in, match and pickup was saved as it happened, so now we can go back and see how the day actually went.

Each year we'll add the new event day here, so we can plan the next one off real numbers.

What you can use it for

  • When kids and mentors actually showed up, and whether each shift had enough mentors
  • Where people waited, for how long, and when the lines started
  • How long checkout took once a parent got there
  • Which stations were busiest and where people had to step in

How to get around:

  1. Pick Board or StoryBoard shows every chart at once. Story walks you through the day one step at a time (Back / Next).
  2. Play with the clockThe bar at the bottom runs the day. Press Play, change the speed, or drag to any time. Every chart moves with it.
  3. Hover anythingEvery dot, line and number tells you what it is when you hover over it.
  4. Click to dig inClick a kid, group or family and it lights up in every chart. Click empty space to open that chart big. Esc to go back.
  5. Not sure what you're looking at?Hit i on any chart for how to read it and what to notice.
