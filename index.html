Shop with a Bulldawg · Event Day Explorer

Every event day happens once. This is how we learn from it.

Each year Shop with a Bulldawg gets one day to do it right: hundreds of kids, mentors and families moving through check-in, matching, shopping and checkout. When it is over, all that is usually left is what people remember.

Starting in 2026, the day keeps its own records. The event sheet that ran check-in, matching and checkout logged every step as it happened. This explorer replays that record, minute by minute, so we can see what actually happened, not what we think happened.

Each year adds another day to compare, so planning gets a little smarter every year.

Questions it helps answer

  • When did kids and mentors really arrive, and did each shift have enough mentors?
  • Where did people wait, for how long, and when did lines form?
  • How long did checkout take once a parent arrived, and what slowed it down?
  • Which stations were busiest, and where did people have to step in?
